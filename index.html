About the school

A government-aided school serving Lira Palwo

Lira Palwo S.S. is listed among Uganda's government-aided secondary schools serving Agago District.

The Uganda Ministry of Education and Sports' 2025 Post-Primary Selection Information Guide gives Lira Palwo S.S. placement code 2404.

The same Ministry guide classifies the school as O-Level and Mixed/Day.

Government education records identify the school with EMIS code 4221.

The school is associated with Lira Palwo in Agago District, Northern Uganda.

Records & administration

Lira Palwo S.S. in Uganda's education records

Historical records explain why some older documents may display Pader or Kitgum while current Ministry records use Agago.

Historical district references

Older Ministry placement documents list Lira Palwo S.S. under Pader, while the current 2025 Ministry guide lists it under Agago District.

The 2025 Ministry guide gives the school as P.O. Patongo, Kitgum. This postal reference should not be interpreted as meaning that the school is currently located in Kitgum District.

Government EMIS-era records identify Lira Palwo S.S. under Agago District and give it institution code 4221.

Important: District and postal references can differ in Ugandan education records because administrative boundaries and postal arrangements do not always use the same names. This page uses Agago District as the current Ministry district classification.
